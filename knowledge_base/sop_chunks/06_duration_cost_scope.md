# Duration, Cost, and Service Scope

Visible notes from page 7:

- Lama pelaksanaan operasi pencarian dan pertolongan paling lama 7 hari dan dapat diperpanjang sesuai ketentuan perundang-undangan.
- Biaya pelaksanaan operasi pencarian dan pertolongan selama 7 hari tidak dipungut biaya.
- Produk pelayanan meliputi pencarian, pertolongan, penyelamatan, dan evakuasi manusia yang menghadapi keadaan darurat/bahaya dalam kecelakaan, bencana, atau kondisi membahayakan manusia.

SARFlow implication:

- Timeline can include operation day count.
- If operation duration approaches or exceeds 7 days, SARFlow may flag "perlu status perpanjangan", but must not decide extension.
- SARFlow should frame itself as administrative support for this service scope.
