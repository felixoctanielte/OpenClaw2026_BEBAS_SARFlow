# Risk Rules

This is administrative triage only. It is not an evacuation decision.

## Red Flags

Raise priority when any of these appear:
- child, elderly person, disability, or other vulnerable victim;
- injury, sickness, hypothermia, drowning risk, or trapped victim;
- extreme weather, heavy rain, flood, landslide risk, strong current;
- victim without logistics, water, warm clothing, or communication;
- SOS signal, last emergency call, or distress message;
- multiple victims;
- last seen/contact time is old or worsening;
- contact with victim/reporter is lost;
- location is remote, mountain, sea, river, forest, cave, or disaster zone.

## Simple Scoring

Start at 0.

- Missing precise location: +2
- Last seen/contact time missing: +2
- Multiple victims: +2
- Injury/sick: +3
- Child/elderly/vulnerable: +3
- Extreme weather/hazard: +2
- No logistics/communication: +2
- SOS/distress signal: +3
- Remote/high-risk terrain: +2
- Reporter contact missing: +2

Priority:
- 0-2: Rendah
- 3-5: Sedang
- 6-10: Tinggi
- 11+: Kritis

Always explain the score in plain Indonesian.
